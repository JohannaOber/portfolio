Hochschule der Medien·Academic project·2024

BIRD Education Platform

Mobile companion app for Germany's national education platform, helping students discover and navigate their options.

My role

UX/UI design·User interviews·Journey mapping·Wireframing

Topic

EdTech·Mobile

Overview

01Problem

A search engine is not enough

Choosing an education path at 17 is overwhelming. BIRD (Bildungsraum Digital), Germany’s federally funded platform for educational programs and institutions, existed only as a web prototype, and a search alone does not help students: they need personalisation and guidance.

02Approach

Personalised and guided

Instead of just listing options, we wanted to create familiarity, personalisation and a companion who meets students on their level and accompanies them through their whole journey.

03Result

A personal space with a hands-on character

A mobile companion that brings all education options into one place: personalised content via the BIRD Wallet, a notebook for favourites and notes, and a chatbot for support on the go. Its paper-inspired design gives it a familiar, hands-on character. Students praised the prototype for clarity and ease of navigation, and the concept was under further consideration as part of the national BIRD platform.

Main decisions

01

Guidance over search

Interviews revealed that students were missing orientation, not options. So personalisation via the BIRD Wallet and the chatbot became the core of the app.

02

A paper-inspired look

The visual language, inspired by paper and handwriting, feels familiar and approachable for younger users and communicates quality through texture and tactility.

Full process

We mapped the journey of education seekers and identified key moments of uncertainty and overwhelm. Interviews with high school students revealed a need for personalisation and guidance: not just a search engine, but a companion that understands where you are in life.

Based on the findings, we defined the core feature set: personalised content via the BIRD Wallet, intelligent search across all programs and institutions, a personal notebook for collecting favourites and notes, and the BIRD chatbot offering real-time support.

We developed a moodboard, paper wireframes for all core flows and iterated toward high-fidelity screens. The visual language, inspired by paper and handwriting, was chosen to feel familiar and approachable for younger users while communicating quality through texture and tactility.

My teammate tested the interactive prototype with a small group of students. Feedback confirmed the value of the personalisation layer and the chatbot, and led to refinements in the notebook and search interactions. The paper-inspired visual style was consistently well-received.
