ELLA – Lifelong Learning with AI

Client

European Commission · University of Oulu, Titanom Solutions GmbH

Year

2025-2026

International Research ProjectSelfregulated Learning

About

ELLA is an international EU research project investigating how self-regulated learning can be sustainably embedded in the everyday classroom instructions, bringing together learning science, AI, and cross-European school expertise.

Challenge

Great in Theory, Stuck in Practice

Teachers across Europe want to bring self-regulated learning into the classroom, but most have no time to read into the methods, let alone apply them. Students, in turn, are rarely even aware of SRL as a concept. Individual support stays out of reach for both sides.

01 Research

Three sources, one coherent picture

Teachers repeatedly said they had no capacity for additional work. That became a research question in itself: not whether they wanted SRL, but what was actually stopping it. To find out, the ELLA team analysed 122 research papers, ran a student survey, and brought together 16 learning experts from across Europe. Students, meanwhile, had no framework for SRL at all.

02 Concept

Turning findings into a process

Research findings were consolidated and prioritized in workshops with the expert group. We built personas for each user group and mapped their pain points along the learning session journey, for both teachers and students, turning insights into a structured design thinking process.

03 Design

From pain points to clear goals

With the main pain points mapped across learning sessions, we ideated on how SRL support could fit into each phase without adding workload. Solution approaches were evaluated in regular feedback rounds with the experts, who also contributed further ideas for different use cases and user groups.

04 Test & Iterate

Built to be validated

The first prototype is currently being evaluated in user tests with teachers and students. The expert community and participating schools across different countries have responded positively, and the project is committed to long-term continuation.

Solution & Impact

A SRL tool for real classrooms

ELLA is still evolving, but the direction is clear and well-received. With €400,000 in EU funding and schools from multiple countries involved from day one, the project is built for real deployment. A long-term commitment from all partners confirms it.
