Akkodis·Internal project·2023

AKKAdemy E-Learning

Redesigning Akkodis's internal learning platform after a company merger, aligning brand identity, improving usability, and rebuilding the component library.

My role

UI audit·Usability review·Component library·Brand application

Topic

E-Learning·Web

Overview

01Problem

New brand, old platform

After the merger of AKKA and Modis into Akkodis, the internal learning platform still reflected the old brand. Beyond a visual refresh, it needed a thorough usability review: inconsistent components and unclear interface patterns were slowing down both learners and content creators.

02Approach

Audit, define, rebuild

Instead of a pure visual refresh, I audited every UI component against the new brand guidelines, prioritised the set for the platform’s core workflows and rebuilt the entire library, with clear naming conventions, layout rules and the Akkodis CI applied consistently throughout.

03Result

A modular foundation for focused learning

The redesigned AKKAdemy platform offers a reduced, functional interface that puts learning content first. The new component library made course creation faster and more consistent, and served as a design foundation for future updates across other Akkodis platforms.
